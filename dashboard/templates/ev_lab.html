How it works
Each card is split into matches where you ran it vs. didn't. Win rates are estimated with a Beta prior (empirical Bayes), then weighted by how often each opponent archetype appears in the meta.

CORE cards (≥75% inclusion) contribute zero delta by definition. TECH slots (<40%) are where the signal lives.
