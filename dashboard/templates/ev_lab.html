how the estimate works

the model compares matches with and without a card. Bayesian smoothing reduces the effect of small samples. opponent frequency sets the field weights.

core cards (≥75% inclusion) are excluded from the list adjustment. their individual comparisons remain visible. tech cards have <40% inclusion. copy-specific estimates appear when enough data exists.

the approximate 90% model interval sums card variance. it excludes baseline uncertainty and dependence between cards. inspect match counts before choosing a tech to test.

both pools match counts and averages source inclusion rates. online match history can extend beyond the period used for current field weights.
