how to read these prices

list cost = Σ copies × the TCGplayer market price of each registered printing (cheapest of its normal / holo / reverse-holo variants). "cheapest printing" swaps a card for an identical reprint: trainers and energy match by name, pokémon only when attacks, abilities and HP match exactly. lists with under 90% of cards priced are left out of the medians so missing prices can't make a deck look cheap.

swap suggestions compare each card's shrunk win-rate effect in this archetype (empirical-Bayes, same model as the tech lab), shown as 80% credible intervals. overlapping intervals mean the data can't tell the two cards apart — not that they play the same. test before you trust.
